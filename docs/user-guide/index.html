First steps

Getting Started

From downloading the app to completing onboarding — get Kursa running in under 5 minutes.

Download & Install

1

Download from the App Store

Search for Kursa on the App Store, or tap the link: Download Kursa on the App Store →. Free to download with a 7-day trial included.

2

Launch on your iPad

Kursa is designed exclusively for iPad. For the best experience use an iPad Air, iPad Pro, or iPad mini running iPadOS 16 or later.

3

Complete the 3-step onboarding

The first time you open Kursa, you'll be guided through Business Type, Template, and Currency selection. This takes about 60 seconds.

Onboarding Steps

Step 1 — Business Type

Choose Restaurant for table service, cafes, and bars. Choose Retail Shop for boutiques, supermarkets, and general stores.

Step 2 — Template

Select a template that matches your business. Templates pre-load example categories and menu items to help you get started faster.

Step 3 — Currency

Choose the currency symbol for your business (e.g. £, $, €). This will appear on all orders, receipts, and reports.

You're in!

After onboarding completes, the app loads your dashboard. Your default login PIN is 1234 — change this immediately in Settings.

Upgrading to Premium

Your 7-day free trial gives you full access. To unlock Kursa permanently:

Floor management

Tables

See your entire floor at a glance. Open tables, track active bills, and manage guests efficiently.

The Tables Screen

Tap Tables in the sidebar to see all your tables. Each card shows:

ElementWhat it means
🟢 Green dotTable is occupied — an active order is open
⚫ Grey dotTable is available
Guest count iconNumber of guests currently seated
TimerHow long the table has been occupied (HH:MM:SS)
Seat capacityMaximum covers for that table

Opening a Table

1

Tap an available table

Tap any table card marked as Available. A modal will open.

2

Enter the number of guests

Use the number pad to enter guest count, then tap Open.

3

The menu opens automatically

You're now in the order screen for that table. Start adding items from the menu.

Table Modes

Dine In

Standard table service. Guests sit, order, and pay at the table. Timer runs from when the table is opened.

Bar

Counter service. Orders can be quick and multiple. Ideal for bars and counter-service cafes.

Takeaway

Collection orders. No table required. Used for walk-in or phone orders collected by the customer.

Switching modes

Use the All / Dine In filter tabs at the top of the tables screen to switch your view. Configure table types in Settings → Tables.

Managing Tables in Settings

To add, edit or delete tables:

Settings → Tables → Add Table

Enter the table name (e.g. "Table 1", "Bar Stool A"), set the seat capacity, and choose the table type. Tables appear immediately on the floor view.

03
Taking orders

Menu & Orders

Browse categories, add items with modifiers, and manage the cart — all without leaving the screen.

The Order Screen Layout

The order screen is split into two panels:

PanelWhat it does
Left — MenuCategory grid at the top, items below. Tap a category to filter items.
Right — CartLive order summary. Shows items, add-ons, subtotal, tax, and total. Checkout buttons at the bottom.

Adding Items to an Order

1

Tap a category

The item grid below updates to show only items in that category. Categories are colour-coded for quick navigation.

2

Tap an item

If the item has no add-ons, it goes straight to the cart. If it has add-ons, a modal opens for customisation.

3

Choose add-ons (if applicable)

Select sides, sauces, sizes, or extras. The total updates live. Tap Add £X.XX to confirm and add to cart.

4

Repeat for more items

Keep adding items. The cart always shows the current running total on the right panel.

Removing an item

In the cart panel, swipe left on an item or tap the delete icon to remove it before completing the order.

Order Actions

ButtonWhat it does
PayComplete the order. Opens the payment screen.
HoldSave the order without payment. Use when guests are still ordering or dining.
PrintPrint the current order ticket to the connected printer.
VoidCancel the entire order. Voided orders are recorded but not counted as sales.

Managing Menu Items

To add, edit, or manage items:

Settings → Items

Each item has a name, price, cost price, quantity/stock level, category, and colour. You can also add add-ons per item (sides, extras, modifiers).

Stock tracking

Set a quantity when creating items to enable stock tracking. When stock falls below the threshold, the item appears in the Low Stock section on the dashboard.

Checkout

Payments

Complete orders with cash or card, print or email receipts, and view your full payment history.

Taking a Cash Payment

1

Tap the Cash button

In the cart, tap Cash to open the cash payment screen. The total owed is shown in large type.

2

Enter the amount received

Use the quick denomination buttons (£10, £20, £40, £60, £80, £100) or type the exact amount using the keypad.

3

Tap Pay £X.XX

The payment completes. A confirmation screen appears showing "Payment Successful".

4

Print or email the receipt

Tap Print Receipt to send to the connected printer, or Email Receipt to send a digital copy to the customer.

Taking a Card Payment

1

Tap the Card button

In the cart, tap Card. Process the payment through your card terminal.

2

Confirm in Kursa

Once your terminal confirms the card payment, tap Pay in Kursa to mark the order as completed.

Card terminals

Kursa records card payments as completed orders. Your physical card terminal (SumUp, Square, iZettle, etc.) handles the actual card transaction separately.

Payment History

Data safety

Backup & Restore

Back up your full setup to Google Drive. Restore instantly on any iPad with the same Google account.

How Backup Works

Kursa backs up everything to your personal Google Drive — not our servers. Only you can access your backups.

What's backed up

Menu items, categories, tables, orders, payments, staff users, shop settings, and taxes/discounts.

Where it's stored

Saved to a Kursa folder in your personal Google Drive. Encrypted and accessible only by your account.

Creating a Backup

1

Go to Settings → Backup

The backup screen shows your Google account status and backup history.

2

Sign in with Google

Tap Sign in with Google and authorise Kursa to access your Google Drive. This is required only once.

3

Tap "Back up now"

Kursa uploads a snapshot of all your data. A confirmation message appears when complete.

Back up regularly

Back up at least once a week, and always before updating the app or replacing your iPad. There is no automatic backup — it must be triggered manually.

Restoring a Backup

1

Install Kursa on the new iPad

Download from the App Store and complete the onboarding steps.

2

Go to Settings → Backup → Restore

Sign in with the same Google account used for backup.

3

Select the backup to restore

Choose the most recent backup from the list. Tap Restore and confirm.

Restore will overwrite existing data

Restoring a backup replaces all current data on the device. Make sure you select the correct backup before confirming.

Hardware

Receipt Printing

Connect your WiFi thermal printer and start printing receipts in under 2 minutes.

Supported Printers

Kursa supports WiFi/LAN thermal printers that use the ESC/POS protocol. Popular compatible models include:

BrandCompatible models
EpsonTM-T20III, TM-T82III, TM-T88VII
Star MicronicsTSP143IIIW, TSP654II
BixolonSRP-350plusIII, SRP-380
Generic ESC/POSMost 80mm WiFi thermal printers
iPad & Bluetooth

iPad supports WiFi/LAN printers only in Kursa. Bluetooth printing is not currently supported. Use a printer connected to the same WiFi network as your iPad.

Connecting Your Printer

1

Connect printer to your WiFi network

Follow your printer manufacturer's instructions to connect it to the same WiFi network your iPad is on.

2

Find the printer's IP address

Print a configuration page from the printer (usually by holding the feed button on startup) — the IP address is printed on it.

3

Go to Settings → Printer

Settings → Printer

Select WiFi/LAN as the connection type.

4

Enter printer details

Enter a Printer Name (e.g. "Front Desk"), the printer's IP Address (e.g. 192.168.1.100), and the Port (default: 9100).

5

Tap Connect Printer

Kursa will attempt to connect. A success message confirms the connection. Try a test print.

Printing Troubleshooting

ProblemFix
Printer not foundCheck printer is powered on and connected to the same WiFi network. Verify the IP address by printing a config page.
Connection failsCheck port number (default 9100). Try restarting the printer and reconnecting.
Print fails mid-jobCheck paper is loaded correctly and printer has no error lights. Reconnect and retry.
Wrong characters printedEnsure the printer is in ESC/POS mode, not line mode. Check printer settings manual.

Help

FAQ & Troubleshooting

Answers to the most common questions about using Kursa.

Getting Started

How do I create my first table?

Go to Settings → Tables, then tap Add Table. Enter the table name, seat capacity, and optional type (Dine In, Bar, or Takeaway).

How do I add menu items?

Go to Settings → Items, tap the plus button, then enter the item name, price, category, and stock details. You can also add modifiers or add-ons later.

How do I switch between Restaurant and Shop mode?

Go to Settings → Shop and update the business mode. Restaurant mode supports tables, waitlist, and held orders. Shop mode is designed for quick sales without table management.

What is the default PIN?

The default PIN is 1234. Change it immediately in Settings → Users → Admin. Select your account and update the PIN.

Orders & Tables

How do I add more items to an existing table?

Tap the occupied table card on the Tables screen. The order screen opens with the current cart — add more items normally. The total updates automatically.

What does Hold mean?

Hold saves the order without taking payment. Use this when a customer is still dining or wants to continue ordering later. Held orders appear on the Orders screen with Pending status.

How do I void an order?

In the cart, tap Void and confirm. Voided orders are recorded in the Orders screen but are not counted as completed sales in your revenue figures.

Can I remove an item from the cart?

Yes. Swipe left on the item in the cart or tap the delete icon before completing payment.

Payments

Can I apply discounts?

Yes. Configure discounts in Settings → Discounts, then apply them during checkout. Discounts can be a fixed £ amount or a percentage.

Can I apply tax?

Yes. Configure tax rates in Settings → Taxes (e.g. VAT 20%). Tax is shown as a separate line on receipts and in order totals.

Can I reprint a receipt?

Yes. Go to Orders, find the completed order, tap it to expand, and tap the print icon. This sends the receipt to your connected printer.

Backup & Data

What happens if I lose my iPad?

If you have a Google Drive backup, install Kursa on a new iPad, sign in with the same Google account, and restore your backup from Settings → Backup → Restore. Everything comes back.

Does Kursa work offline?

Yes. All data is stored locally on your iPad. You can take orders, process payments, and print receipts without an internet connection. Internet is only required for Google Drive backup and restore.

Is my data shared with Kursa/Suftnet?

No. All app data stays on your device or your personal Google Drive. We do not collect, access, or store your business data on our servers.

Troubleshooting

The app is slow or unresponsive

Close the app fully from the iPad multitasking view and reopen it. If the problem persists, restart your iPad. Make sure you are running the latest version from the App Store.

An order has disappeared

Check the Orders screen and use the status filters (All, Progress, Pending, Completed, Cancelled). Orders are never automatically deleted. If still missing, contact support.

The printer is connected but not printing

Check that the printer has paper loaded, the power is on, and it's still connected to the WiFi network. Go to Settings → Printer and tap Connect Printer again, then retry.

How do I contact support?

Email us at support@suftnet.com with your device model, iOS version, a description of the issue, and any screenshots. We typically respond within 24 hours on business days.
